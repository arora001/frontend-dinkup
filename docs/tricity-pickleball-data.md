## 1. Executive Summary: The Chandigarh Tricity Pickleball Ecosystem

The Chandigarh Tricity metropolitan area (Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, and Zirakpur) represents one of Northern India's fastest-growing racket sports hubs. With a high concentration of retired defense personnel, active tech professionals from QuarkCity and Bestech, university athletes from Panjab University, and an affluent health-conscious populace, pickleball is experiencing exponential adoption:
- **Morning Cohorts (06:00 – 10:30):** Dominated by retirees, doctors, entrepreneurs, and fitness enthusiasts favoring soft kitchen control, unhurried rallies, and post-game social coffees.
- **Midday Lulls (11:00 – 16:00):** High summer temperatures lead to court maintenance, private coaching clinics, or indoor climate-controlled play.
- **Evening & Floodlit Prime Hours (16:30 – 23:00):** Peak capacity period. Techies from IT City Mohali, corporate teams, and young competitive players (DUPR 3.5 – 4.5) battle under 300-lux LED floodlights.
- **Open Games & WhatsApp Culture:** Racket culture in the Tricity thrives on informal WhatsApp groups where games are organized via seat splits (e.g. ₹150–₹225 per player for a 4-seat doubles reservation).
